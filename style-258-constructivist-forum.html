Human reference

Constructivism makes the interface feel mobilized: direct, geometric, urgent, and built for collective action. The visual system follows the movement’s actual principles: rodchenko-inspired diagonals, photomontage energy, limited color, and bold sans serif type. It gives teams a recognizable point of view without pretending that a color swap is a new design language.
