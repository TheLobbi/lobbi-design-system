Human reference

De Stijl turns civic information into a clear composition of line, plane, and primary color. The visual system follows the movement’s actual principles: mondrian’s primary-color geometry, orthogonal grids, and reductive composition. It gives teams a recognizable point of view without pretending that a color swap is a new design language.
