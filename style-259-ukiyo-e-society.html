Human reference

Ukiyo-e brings a calm, narrative surface through flat planes, framed scenes, and deliberate negative space. The visual system follows the movement’s actual principles: japanese woodblock composition, flat color fields, wave contours, and quiet asymmetry. It gives teams a recognizable point of view without pretending that a color swap is a new design language.
