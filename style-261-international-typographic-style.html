Human reference

The International Typographic Style makes complex information legible through a strict grid, clear hierarchy, and purposeful restraint. The visual system follows the movement’s actual principles: swiss grid discipline, sans serif hierarchy, asymmetry, and objective information design. It gives teams a recognizable point of view without pretending that a color swap is a new design language.
